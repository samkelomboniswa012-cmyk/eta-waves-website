PRIVACY MATTERS

Player data deserves care.

Before launching publicly, ETA Waves should publish an approved privacy notice that identifies who manages the information, why it is collected, who can access it, how long it is kept and how a student can request a correction or deletion where applicable.

This prototype uses browser storage only. It does not send information to a central database, and its local demo IDs are not permanent or shared between devices. Do not use it to collect real student registrations until a secure backend, access permissions, backups and appropriate consent/safeguarding procedures have been implemented.
